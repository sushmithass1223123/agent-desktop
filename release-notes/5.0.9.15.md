## Changelog


### Features

- **EmailWorkbenchWidget**
    - "Assigned To" label changed to "Assigned To (by agentID)"
    - User can now switch between original and replied email while viewing sentitem or drafts email


### Fixes

- **EmailControlsWidget**
    - Sent items and Draft emails weren't previewed for emails that were composed
    - Workbench email preview wasn’t loading in chrome
    - Priority wasn't visible
- **EmailTemplateSelectorComponent**
    - Email body wasn't scrollable
- **ActiveAgentsWidget**
  -  Missing scroll for agent list
- **InteractionDetailsWidget**
  -  Comments were not searchable
- **AuxTimerWidget**
  -  timer was resetting when left idle for longtime


### Refactor
- **EmailWorkbenchWidget**
  -  Removed tinymce and other optimizations
